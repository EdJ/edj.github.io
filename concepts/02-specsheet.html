Note 01 Design intent

Grooveboxes lock an instrument to a track. Trackers don't — and that is the single most useful thing a tracker does. Jolt keeps the tracker's per-step instrument assignment and throws away the column of hex.

The other half is knowing when to stop. An all-at-once view of every voice was built, then removed: past a certain song complexity nobody could follow what was happening. Legibility over density, both times.
